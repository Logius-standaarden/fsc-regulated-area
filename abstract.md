Regulated Area is an extension on the Federated Service Connectivity (FSC) standard [FSC Core](https://github.com/Logius-standaarden/fsc-core) and the FSC Properties extension. It can only be used in addition to the FSC Core specification.

Within an FSC Group there could be additional membership restrictions to either the entire Group or a subsection of this Group, this extension calls this a Regulated Area.
Peers who want to participate in a Regulated Area, this means to publish and consume services within the Regulated Area, must be a member of the Regulated Area.
The Regulated Area extension introduces a Peer Registration Grant Type as a means for Peers to register with a Regulated Area. It uses the Properties extension to define the scope of the Regulated Area.

A Regulated Area will also make it possible to automate processes within the Regulated Area. For example; Service providers can automatically accept connection requests based on membership of the Regulated Area.

The Regulated Area extension introduces the Peer Registration Grant Type and describes the Content and structure of this new Grant, as well as new endpoints and interactions regarding the new Peer Registration Grant.
It also describes the manner in which Peers can interact with other Peers and Services in a Regulated Area. How, verification, validation and revocation of Peers in a Regulated Area will work.
